Entry for the [The Absurd Clause Contest](https://make.fun/bounty/u-donrulezz-the-absurd-clause-contest-035cc0f1) bounty on make.fun.

One absurd clause buried deep in a terms-of-service agreement, in deadpan legal boilerplate: by using the service, you grant the Company the right to attend your funeral, sit in the front row, and rate the catering out of ten.

`main.ts` writes the clause to `/output/entry.txt` (one line, 134 characters).

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```
